Disconnect from QuickBooks

How to revoke Sud Scrub Analytics's access to your QuickBooks Online company file.

An Authorized User can revoke the Application's access at any time. There are two equivalent ways to do this:

Option 1 — From Intuit App Store

  1. Sign in to https://appcenter.intuit.com/myapps.
  2. Locate Sud Scrub Analytics in the list of connected apps.
  3. Click the menu next to the app name and select Disconnect.
  4. Confirm when prompted. The connection is revoked immediately and the Application can no longer access the company file.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the operator

  1. Email manning.field@gmail.com and request that the connection to your company file be disconnected and all cached data deleted.
  2. The operator will revoke the tokens and delete cached data within seven (7) business days, and will confirm by reply email.

Disconnection immediately stops all future API access. Any reports already delivered to the Company remain in the Company's records.

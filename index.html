Sud Scrub Analytics

Internal financial analytics and cashflow forecasting for SUDScrub.

About

Sud Scrub Analytics is a private, internal application used by SUDScrub operations to pull read-only accounting data from QuickBooks Online for the purpose of financial analysis, EBITDA reporting, and cash flow forecasting. It is not offered to the public and is operated solely for SUDScrub and its authorized consultants.

Scope

  • Read-only access to QuickBooks Online accounting data (company file, chart of accounts, transactions, reports, balance sheets).
  • No write-back, no bill pay, no payroll, no payment collection.
  • Data is used exclusively by SUDScrub internal operations and its retained financial consultants.
